A bobber is useful when fish are shallow, visibly cruising, feeding above weeds, or when you need to hold bait at a known depth. A slip bobber is preferable when the bait depth becomes too deep for a fixed float.

## Rigging
`Main line → float → leader → split shot → swivel → leader → #4 hook w/bait`

## Weight
Use a split shot weight.

## Bait
Run a worm up the hook to the eye, or use PowerBait dough, nugget, or egg

## Technique
- Start around 3–5 feet deep when fish are shallow
- Go deeper if there are no bites and deeper water is nearby
- Keep the bait above weed tops rather than burying it in vegetation
- Cast near shade lines, weed edges, dock edges, shoreline drop-offs, visible fish activity, and mildly wind-blown water
- Give the bobber an occasional small twitch, then let the bait settle.
