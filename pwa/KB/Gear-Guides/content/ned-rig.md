A small mushroom-style jig head with a short, buoyant soft-plastic worm or stick bait. The bait stands upright on bottom when at rest, mimicking a small baitfish or craw nibbling on bottom.

# Use
- Best for cold, clear, or high-pressure conditions, with bottom-oriented bass that are feeding on small prey or crustaceans around deep or mid-depth structure like gravel flats, rocky points, or drop-offs.
- Use year-round, but especially effective in cold water or post-frontal conditions when bass are ignoring larger baits
- The Ned rig allows you to fish at any depth in the water column depending on jig head weight — lighter for shallow, heavier for deep

# Gear
- Rod: Medium power
- Reel: Spinning
- Line: 12 lb. braid w/ 8 lb. 5' long fluoro leader. Use a longer leader for deep water.
- Hook: 1/0 – 2/0 mushroom-style jig head. 1/8 oz for shallow water, 3/16 oz for 6–12 ft, 1/4 oz for deeper edges or wind.
- Lure: Small plastic stick baits
- Knot: Tie directly to leader w/Palomar knot. Use a Loop knot in cold or pressured water to get maximum finesse.

# Technique
## Most to least effective:
1. Pop the Bottom: Cast and let  the lure sink to the bottom, then pop it up slightly and let it fall back  down as you reel it back in. Go slow. On the drop, shake the rod to  provide added action and entice more strikes.
2. Swim and Glide: Cast and swim  the bait by reeling slowly, then pause to allow the bait to glide down.  Repeat as you retrieve. On the drop, shake the rod to provide added action  and entice more strikes. Good in deeper water.
3. Drag and Dead Stick: Cast and  let the lure sink to the bottom, shaking the rod on the drop. Drag along  the bottom with a long slow sweep of the rod and pause. Reel in some and  repeat as you retrieve. Good over rocks because tip of hook will knock on  rocks and attract bass.
4. Straight Retrieve: Cast and  reel back in slowly to impart a natural swimming action
