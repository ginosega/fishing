Pass the end through the eye twice and form a complete loop alongside the standing line. Working inside the loop, wrap the end around both lines five times. Lubricate, tighten so the loop spirals, and slide the knot to the desired loop size, or all the way down to the eye if no loop is desired. Pull on the tag end and less hard on the standing line (keep a finger or other object inside if leaving a loop) and trim the end.

The modified uni knot initially forms an adjustable loop that when tightened becomes fixed in proportion to the degree of tightening. The knot undergoes a transformation as it is tightened; the outer wraps become internal and vice versa. When used for joining two lines, each knot is tied around the other’s standing end - this is known as the double uni knot.

Note that the first two pictures above only show the line going through the eye once - this is the single uni knot. Going through the eye twice is the modified or improved uni knot, which is much stronger.

When using any loop knot with a lure, the knot must be inspected regularly for wear or fraying of the loop.
