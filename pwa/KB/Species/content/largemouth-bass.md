Largemouth bass are a primary target in shallow vegetation, docks, protected water, and cover-oriented areas. Current location and technique pages contain the detailed water-specific and presentation guidance.

Video: [Life of the Largemouth Bass](https://youtu.be/dNG8z_IxlJ0?si=hnJsjq8gUNrOVAal)

For general location and seasonal behavior, see [Bass Behavior and Habitat](kb://bass-behavior-and-habitat). For presentations and lure selection, see [Bass Fishing Techniques](kb://bass-fishing-techniques), including [Topwater Fishing](kb://topwater-fishing) when fish are feeding upward or using shallow cover.
