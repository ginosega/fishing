Smallmouth bass commonly relate to rock, gravel, points, drop-offs, offshore structure, and roaming baitfish, although they can also feed shallow or at the surface.

For general location and seasonal behavior, see [Bass Behavior and Habitat](kb://bass-behavior-and-habitat). For presentation choices, see [Bass Fishing Techniques](kb://bass-fishing-techniques) and [Topwater Fishing](kb://topwater-fishing) when smallmouth are chasing bait near the surface.
