Fish dawn and dusk at boat-launch docks, rocky shorelines, points, riprap, shade lines, and quick drop-offs.

For broader presentation guidance, see [Bass Fishing Techniques](kb://bass-fishing-techniques), [Topwater Fishing](kb://topwater-fishing), and [Trout Fishing Techniques](kb://technique-trout-fishing).

# Smallmouth Gear
- Ned rig: green pumpkin, 1/10-1/6 oz.
- Drop shot: 3-4" shad/minnow/worm bait.
- Tube jig: green pumpkin or smoke/purple, dragged slowly.
- Small paddletail: 2.8-3.3" on 1/8-1/4 oz jighead.
- Small jerkbait or [topwater](kb://topwater-fishing) first/last light.

# Trout Gear
- 1/8-1/4 oz Kastmaster or spoon.
- Small inline spinner.
- Slip-sinker rig with PowerBait or worm for relaxing bite chance.
