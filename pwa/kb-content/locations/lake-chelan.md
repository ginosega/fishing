OneNote notes:

- Fish early morning/evening, dawn/dusk.
- Fish boat-launch docks, rocky shoreline, points, riprap, shade lines, and quick drop-offs.

Smallmouth gear:

- Ned rig: green pumpkin, 1/10-1/6 oz.
- Drop shot: 3-4" shad/minnow/worm bait.
- Tube jig: green pumpkin or smoke/purple, dragged slowly.
- Small paddletail: 2.8-3.3" on 1/8-1/4 oz jighead.
- Small jerkbait or topwater first/last light.

Trout gear:

- 1/8-1/4 oz Kastmaster or Little Cleo-style spoon.
- Small inline spinner.
- Slip-sinker rig with PowerBait or worm for relaxing bite chance.

## Before fishing

For every trip, recheck:

- Fish Washington regulations.
- Emergency rules.
- Park/dock signage.
- Parking/launch restrictions.
- Species-specific retention rules.
