# Rigging

Main line → small
sliding weight → bead → swivel → 3-4' leader → dodger → 18-30" leader →
lure (Wedding Ring / hoochie / small spinner, with egg)

A dodger adds flash
and side-to-side action, which helps when you are kayak trolling. It also makes
your Wedding Ring, hoochie, or spinner more effective. Good starting colors:

- silver
- chrome
- watermelon
- UV/pink/orange

# Weight

Use 1/2 oz.
generally, 1 to 2 oz. to keep lure deeper.

# Lures

Mack’s Wedding Ring
trolling spinner or small kokanee/trout hoochie in white/pink/orange/UV colors.
Tip with a small piece of nightcrawler, PowerBait egg, or shoepeg corn.
