A finesse rig where
the hook is tied 8-18" above a weight, keeping the bait suspended just off
the bottom.

# Use

- Best for deeper water (8–40+
  ft), suspended fish that won’t chase bottom baits, and clear water where
  subtle action and precise placement matters
- Use in summer through fall
  when fish are deep or suspended, around structure (rocks, brush, humps,
  ledges), and on sonar-marked fish or finesse situations.

# Gear

- Rod: 7' medium light power
  with a sensitive tip
- Reel: Spinning
- Line: 15-20 lb. braided
  mainline to 6-8 lb. fluorocarbon leader
- Hook: Size 2, 1, or 1/0 Drop
  Shot hook
- Knot:

- Run tip of line line through
  front (point side) of hook, extending far enough for the distance between
  the hook and weight. Make a loop behind the hook and push the hook
  sideways around the line and through the loop 4 times. Wet the line and
  pull tight. See video: [Drop Shot - Tying Knot](https://youtu.be/xZVBaLus368?si=vBu4cfsA0gAsvF9X&t=207)
- Also tie a knot to attach
  the weight at the end of the line

- Weight:

- Rule of thumb is to use the
  lightest weight you can while still feeling the bottom
- Try 1/8 oz. for shallow (up
  to 10') & calm water, 3/16- 1/4 oz. for medium depth (10-20'), 3/8
  oz. for deep (20-35'), 1/2 oz. for very deep (35'+) water
- Use cylinder weights for
  all-around conditions, ball weights for sandy/mossy bottoms and teardrop
  for rocky
- Length between weight and
  hook: 12-18" is standard, 18-24" when fishing over grass.
  Adjust when using a fish locator. Goal is to have the lure at the level
  where the fish are.

# Lure

- Small worms, minnows, or
  finesse plastics
- Finesse plastics: Z-Man Trick
  Shotz 3.5", Finesse Shadz and TRD Gobyz, all green pumpkin
- Attach with nose hook; insert
  hook below head, leave hook point just inside tip of bait. See video: [Drop Shot - Nose Hook](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xZVBaLus368&t=302s).

# Technique

- Cast out, let weight hit
  bottom (line will slacken), let it sit and gently shake the slack line to
  wiggle the bait without lifting the weight.
- If no hits then drag the
  weight in across the bottom until you feel it hit something solid, and
  then stop and work the bait there for a while.
- Setting the hook: Don't be
  too violent because this is a light line and rod. Lift up and reel in the
  line.
