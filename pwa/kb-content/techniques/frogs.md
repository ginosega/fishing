A topwater lure
designed to imitate a real frog and slide across vegetation or skip under cover
to draw surface strikes. Good for bass and pike in shallow, weedy environments.

# Use

- In summer through early fall,
  when surface temps are high and fish are feeding near the surface
- In heavy vegetation (lily
  pads, grass mats, slop) - their weedless design allows fishing where other
  lures can’t go
- In shallow cover near banks,
  wood, or reeds, where frogs live naturally
- In low light (early morning,
  evening, overcast) when predators hunt the surface more aggressively

# Gear

- Rod: 7’ – 7’6” heavy, fast
- Reel: High-speed baitcaster
  (7.1:1 – 8.1:1)
- Line: 50–65 lb. braid, no
  leader
- Knot: Palomar - reliable and
  strong for braid; won’t slip or loosen in cover. Tie directly to lure.

# Technique

- Walk the Frog: Twitch rod tip
  rhythmically with slack in line to make the frog glide side-to-side. Best
  in open pockets or sparse vegetation.
- Straight Retrieve: Reel
  slowly and steadily to make legs kick (for solid-body frogs). Great when
  covering water quickly.
- Pop-and-Pause: Pop the frog,
  let it rest for 1–2 seconds, repeat. Imitates a resting frog; deadly when
  fish are cautious
- Burn-and-Kill: Quick burst of
  retrieve, then pause suddenly. Triggers reaction bites from followers.
- When you see a blowup, wait a
  second before setting the hook — let the fish grab the frog first

# Lures

- Hollow-body frog:

- Soft, collapsible body with
  hidden double hooks that sit flush against the sides
- Designed for heavy
  vegetation — can be fished across lily pads and mats without snagging
- E.g., Booyah Pad Crasher,
  Spro Bronzeye 65, Scum Frog Trophy Series

- Popping frog:

- Hollow-body but with a
  cupped mouth that spits and chugs
- Ideal for open water or
  along edges where you want more sound/action to draw fish
- E.g., Strike King Popping
  Perch, LiveTarget Hollow Body Popper Frog, SPRO Bronzeye Poppin’ Frog

- Soft plastic (solid-body)
  frog:

- Kicking legs, rigged on a
  single or double hook
- Burned across open water or
  sparse grass & weeds for reaction strikes
- E.g., Zoom Horny Toad,
  Stanley Ribbit, Strike King Rage Toad

- Mods:

- Legs should be about the
  same length as the body - trim them if they are too long, as this will
  prevent the frog from walking
- Hooks that are tight next to
  the body may prevent hook-ups; bend them away slightly if this is
  happening
- Boil the frogs to soften the
  plastic if not getting hook-ups
