A topwater lure designed to imitate a real frog and slide across vegetation or skip under cover to draw surface strikes. Good for bass and pike in shallow, weedy environments.

Source resource: [Basics of Frog Fishing and How to Fish Hollow-body Frog Lures](https://youtu.be/czTtqyDKDcA?si=h9bn95vZ-8Y_xcI1).

### Use

- In summer through early fall, when surface temperatures are high and fish are feeding near the surface.
- In heavy vegetation (lily pads, grass mats, slop)—their weedless design allows fishing where other lures can’t go.
- In shallow cover near banks, wood, or reeds, where frogs live naturally.
- In low light (early morning, evening, overcast) when predators hunt the surface more aggressively.

### Gear

- Rod: 7'–7'6" heavy, fast.
- Reel: High-speed baitcaster (7.1:1–8.1:1).
- Line: 50–65 lb braid, no leader.
- Knot: [Palomar](kb://knot-palomar)—reliable and strong for braid; won’t slip or loosen in cover.

### Technique

- **Walk the Frog:** Twitch the rod tip rhythmically with slack in the line to make the frog glide side-to-side. Best in open pockets or sparse vegetation.
- **Straight Retrieve:** Reel slowly and steadily to make the legs kick (for solid-body frogs). Great when covering water quickly.
- **Pop-and-Pause:** Pop the frog, let it rest for 1–2 seconds, repeat. Imitates a resting frog; deadly when fish are cautious.
- **Burn-and-Kill:** Quick burst of retrieve, then pause suddenly. Triggers reaction bites from followers.
- When you see a blowup, wait a second before setting the hook—let the fish grab the frog first.

### Bait

**Hollow-body frog**

- Soft, collapsible body with hidden double hooks that sit flush against the sides.
- Designed for heavy vegetation—can be fished across lily pads and mats without snagging.
- Examples: Booyah Pad Crasher, Spro Bronzeye 65, Scum Frog Trophy Series.

**Popping frog**

- Hollow-body but with a cupped mouth that spits and chugs.
- Ideal for open water or along edges where you want more sound/action to draw fish.
- Examples: Strike King Popping Perch, LiveTarget Hollow Body Popper Frog, SPRO Bronzeye Poppin’ Frog.

**Soft-plastic (solid-body) frog**

- Kicking legs, rigged on a single or double hook.
- Burned across open water or sparse grass and weeds for reaction strikes.
- Examples: Zoom Horny Toad, Stanley Ribbit, Strike King Rage Toad.

### Mods

- Legs should be about the same length as the body. Trim them if they are too long, as this will prevent the frog from walking.
- Hooks that are tight next to the body may prevent hookups; bend them away slightly if this is happening.
- Boil the frogs to soften the plastic if not getting hookups.
