A topwater wire-frame lure that looks like a spinnerbait but creates noise via a rotating propeller blade on the wire arm that attracts predatory fish through sound and vibration. Good for largemouth and smallmouth bass, and occasional pike and muskie.

### Use

- For fast, surface-level retrieves that imitate a fleeing baitfish or struggling creature on top of the water.
- In warm water (60°F+) when bass are most aggressive.
- In low light (morning, evening, overcast) when bass are feeding shallow.
- In wind and chop—the buzzing propeller will cut through the surface sound.
- In shallow cover and murky water—the lure will ride on top of the cover and its sound will help fish locate it.
- Don't use in cold water because fish are sluggish and won't react, or in clear water where they can get a good look at it.

### Gear

- Rod: 7–7'6", medium-heavy, fast.
- Reel: Baitcaster with fast retrieve (7.4:1).
- Line: Use 20–30 lb braid in less heavy cover, 30–40 lb in heavy cover. Optionally use a short fluorocarbon leader for less line visibility.
- Tie directly to the lure with a [Palomar knot](kb://knot-palomar).
- Do not use a snap or swivel: buzzbaits do not spin; these add weight that can pull the bait down, and they can catch weeds.
- Add a frog, swimbait, or grub trailer to increase lift and bulk, either to the main hook or a [trailer hook](https://youtu.be/T_GlH_71xGA?si=kW-vykKcVP0jX2Ot).

### Technique

- Start retrieving as soon as it hits the water; don't let it sink.
- Standard retrieve: Keep rod tip up and reel just fast enough to keep the blade buzzing on the surface.
- Retrieve faster in warm, shallow water to provoke aggressive strikes.
- Retrieve slower when fish are timid or water is colder—just barely fast enough to keep it churning.
- When fish are following but not striking, pause briefly to let fish react, then restart buzzing ("stop-and-go").

### Color

- White / shad: some say use on clear water or bright light; others say use on cloudy days.
- Black: some say use in low light or night; others say use on sunny days.
- Chartreuse: stained or muddy water.

### Baits

- Booyah Buzz: affordable, reliable.
- Strike King: great balance, strong hook.
- War Eagle: excellent sound, easy plane-up.
- Jackall Firecracker: unique “clacking” blade noise.
- Megabass Jamaica Boa: premium.
- Greenfish Hammerhead.
