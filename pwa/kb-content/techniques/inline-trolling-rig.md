# Rigging
Main line → torpedo
weight with swivels at each end→ 3-4' leader → lure

# Weight
Use 1/2 oz.
generally, 1/4 oz. to keep lure shallower in mornings & evenings, 3/4 or 1
oz. to keep lure deeper.

# Lures
Mack’s Wedding Ring
trolling spinner or small kokanee/trout hoochie in white/pink/orange/UV colors.
Tip with a small piece of nightcrawler, PowerBait egg, or shoepeg corn.
