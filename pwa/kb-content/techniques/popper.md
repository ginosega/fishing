A hard-plastic realistic lure that imitates baitfish rising to the surface. Good for shallow bass.

### Use

- In spring when salmon are spawning; they will attack this fish to protect their beds.
- Also in the fall and in low light or dark water.
- On calm days since it doesn't make much noise.
- Fish it over submerged vegetation and near cover such as wood and docks.

### Gear

- Rod/Reel: Medium-fast spinning—this is a lighter technique requiring more tip action.
- Line: 30 lb braid, moss color.
- Knot: Tie directly to the lure using a [Palomar knot](kb://knot-palomar) or Clinch knot if the lure has a split ring; use a [loop knot](kb://knot-loop-non-slip-loop) if it does not.

### Technique

- "Pop" by twitching the rod tip downward. The lure should spit water and make a bubble as it pops.
- Pause between pops to let the ripples dissipate.
- Reel in slack and repeat.

### Bait

- Rebel Pop-R.
- 6th Sense popper.
