## Braid-to-leader knots

| Setup | Preferred knot | Easier field knot | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 15 lb braid to 8 lb fluoro | FG knot | Alberto | Spinning/finesse; 6-8 ft leader common |
| 30 lb braid to 12 lb fluoro | FG knot | Alberto | Baitcaster; 4-7 ft leader common; avoid burying bulky knots deep in spool |

Double Uni works but is bulkier.

## Direct tie / snap / swivel guidance

| Lure type | Connection | Swivel use |
|---|---|---|
| Crankbaits | Leader to small crankbait snap | No swivel normally |
| Hard jerkbaits | Leader to snap or direct to split ring | No swivel normally |
| Spoons | Leader to small snap | Swivel optional if twisting/rolling |
| Inline spinners | Direct tie or tiny snap to lure | Ball-bearing swivel up the leader preferred |
| Wedding Ring / trolling spinner | Direct to rig/lure | Swivel strongly preferred |
| Spinnerbait | Usually direct tie | No swivel/snap-swivel |
| Chatterbait | Usually direct tie | No swivel/snap-swivel |
| Wacky/Ned/drop shot | Direct tie | No snap/swivel except drop-shot rig hardware if desired |
| Topwater | Usually direct tie or small snap depending lure | Avoid fluorocarbon leader for floating topwater if it pulls bait down |

## Trout rigging examples

### PowerBait bottom rig

`main line -> 1/2 oz egg sinker -> glass bead -> swivel -> 18-30 inch leader -> #8 hook -> PowerBait / Trout Nugget / Power Eggs`

### Inline spinner rig

`main line -> fluoro leader -> ball-bearing swivel -> 18-30 inch fluoro leader -> Panther Martin or Mepps`

### Kayak trolling weight rig

`main line -> 1/2 oz swiveling torpedo weight -> leader/snap or swivel -> lure`

For dodger rigs, the typical order is:

`main line -> trolling weight -> swivel -> dodger -> 18-30 inch leader -> Wedding Ring / hoochie / small trolling spinner`
