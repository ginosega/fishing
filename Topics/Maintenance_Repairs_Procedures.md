## Daiwa Exceler LT spinning reel maintenance

OneNote linked [Greasing and Oiling Your Spinning Reel](https://youtu.be/vkCUgxwUtqU?si=cZehY_eKmqr9HXdn).

Historical routine guidance:

- After freshwater trips: wipe exterior with damp cloth, especially spool lip, bail arm, line roller, handle knob, and reel foot.
- Dry before storage.
- Every few trips: apply one tiny drop of reel oil to line roller, bail pivots, handle knob shaft/bearing area, and very lightly to main shaft under spool if dry.
- After rain/sand/dirt/salt/brackish exposure: tighten drag, rinse gently with low-pressure freshwater, shake/wipe dry, loosen drag, air dry shaded/ventilated.
- Do not blast with pressure water or submerge.
- Do not oil drag washers.
- Do not flood reel with oil.
- Avoid WD-40/3-in-1/automotive oils as primary reel lubricant.

## Shimano SLX DC XT maintenance notes

Historical notes from translated Japanese manual/discussion:

- Apply tiny oil drops only at indicated oil points.
- Avoid oiling drag/electronics/DC brake unit.
- Manual diagrams included oil callouts and warnings about clutch return during casting.
- Longer handle or heavy knobs may make clutch return during casting more likely.
- Cast with handle side oriented up or down rather than sticking sideways when relevant to prevent accidental handle rotation/clutch return.

## Pflueger President push-button/spincast reel maintenance

Recommended basic service after years of no maintenance:

1. Remove and discard old line.
2. Unscrew/remove front cone.
3. Wipe inside cone, spool face, center shaft, pickup pins, and line guide hole.
4. Use mild soapy water only for grime; dry fully.
5. Check pickup pins by pressing button and turning handle; they should retract/extend smoothly.
6. Add one tiny drop of reel oil to sticky pickup-pin mechanism if needed.
7. Add one small drop of reel oil to center shaft.
8. Oil handle knobs if squeaky/stiff.
9. Check drag after re-spooling.
10. Optional: open gear side only if comfortable; remove dried grease and apply a small amount of reel grease to gear teeth.
11. Re-spool with 6 lb monofilament, not overfilled; stop about 1/8 inch below spool lip.

General rules:

- Replace old spincast mono before use.
- Check braid-to-leader knots for wear before each trip.
- Inspect leaders after catching fish, snagging, or fishing rocks/wood.
- Retie vibration lures often because spinnerbait/chatterbait/crankbait/inline-spinner vibration stresses knots.

## Hook and lure maintenance

- [Hook sharpener](https://a.co/d/04J8axAa) owned per OneNote.
- Check treble hooks for sharpness and rust.
- Rinse and dry lures/hooks after use, especially if used in brackish/saltwater.
- Replace damaged split rings/hooks as needed; OneNote candidate kit mentions size 2-3 stainless split rings.
- Use hook covers/keepers in kayak storage to protect hands and rods.
