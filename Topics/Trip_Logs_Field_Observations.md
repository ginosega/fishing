## Future logging guidance

After each trip, capture:

- date/time;
- location and exact access point;
- platform: kayak/bank/dock/boat;
- water temperature if known;
- weather, wind, clarity, and lake level if relevant;
- lures/bait/rigs used;
- depth/structure;
- bites/catches/no-bite observations;
- sonar observations;
- what to change next time.
