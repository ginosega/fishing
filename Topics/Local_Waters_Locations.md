## Lake Washington

### General

Common user targets: bass and coastal cutthroat trout. Kokanee can be present/seasonal in some contexts but should be treated as a more specialized target and regulations should be checked.

### North end / Kenmore / Log Boom Park

Known access/use:

- Kenmore boat launch used for kayak access.
- Log Boom Park public dock/pier has been discussed for bank/dock fishing.
- North end includes Sammamish River / slough influence, weed edges, channel edges, and shoreline/dock structure.

Tactics:

- Bass: wacky Senko, soft jerk shad, spinnerbait, chatterbait, topwater in low light, drop shot/Ned on harder/deeper edges.
- Trout/cutthroat: troll/cast Flicker Shad, Kastmaster, Dick Nite, Panther Martin, Mepps; focus on contour edges, bait, creek/river influence.

User observation:

- Evening kayak trip near the weed edges/channel where the Sammamish River meets the lake: fished wacky worm and spinnerbait from 8:30-9:30 p.m.; saw small fish rising for flying insects; no bites. Future adjustment: use subtler baitfish presentations such as soft jerk shad, drop shot Finesse ShadZ, small spoons/jerkbaits, or topwater only if predatory strikes are visible.

## Lake Sammamish

### General

User fishes/considers both bass and trout. Bass planning has focused on prespawn/spawn/postspawn structure, weed edges, docks, benches, and creek influence.

Access notes to preserve/audit:

- Lake Sammamish State Park: main public trailered boat launch on south end.
- Idylwood Park: important north-end hand/kayak access.
- Luke McRedmond Landing: Sammamish River/slough transition access.
- Sammamish Landing: limited paddling access/rest stop; not necessarily a start/end point.

Tactics:

- Bass: drop shot, Ned rig, wacky Senko, soft jerk shad, spinnerbait, chatterbait, small swimbait.
- Trout/cutthroat: troll/cast small baitfish lures along points, contour edges, creek influence, and bait marks.

User observation:

- Possible smallmouth spawning/structure area about half mile north of State Park launch on east side: flat jutting out around 12 ft deep, drops on three sides, small fish observed. Larger smallmouth may hold on nearby deeper edges/drop-offs.

## Mayfield Lake / Ike Kinswa State Park

Context:

- User planned camping at Ike Kinswa State Park with kayaks.
- Prior plans targeted rainbow trout, bass, and incidental tiger muskie awareness.

Likely/previously discussed fish:

- Rainbow trout
- Kokanee
- Largemouth bass
- Tiger muskie
- Northern pikeminnow
- Other warmwater/panfish possible but needs verification

Tactics:

### Morning

- You/baitcaster: Flicker Shad 5, Pearl White; troll contour edges/points/cove mouths.
- Jacob/spinning: Kastmaster, Panther Martin, Dick Nite, RipStop/Husky Jerk as conditions dictate.

### Midday

- Trout: deeper trolling with 1/2 oz torpedo weight + Flicker Shad/Kastmaster/Dick Nite.
- Bass: shade/wood/docks/outside weed edges with Power Jerk Shad, wacky Senko, jig + Rage Craw, DT06.
- Scout evening spots with Navionics/Helix.

### Evening

- Bass around cover: soft jerk shad, spinnerbait, Whopper Plopper 60, wacky Senko, DT06.
- Trout if rising/active: Kastmaster, Dick Nite, Panther Martin, small jerkbaits.

User observation:

- Northern pikeminnow caught trolling Berkley Flicker Shad 5, Pearl White.

Bank-fishing plan at state park:

- Still-fish PowerBait on 1/2 oz egg sinker + bead + swivel + 18-30 inch leader + #8 hook.
- Bobber fish bait 2-5 ft down near weeds/shade/activity.
- Actively cast Kastmaster, Dick Nite, Panther Martin, Husky Jerk, Rapala Original Floating, or bass lures while bait soaks.

## Lake Chelan

Context:

- User fished/asked about Lake Chelan State Park dock/shore.

Prior guidance:

- Boat/trolling lake for kokanee/lake trout/Chinook, but from park shore/dock target smallmouth and trout.
- Use Ned rig, drop shot, tubes, small paddletail, small jerkbait/topwater for smallmouth.
- Use spoons/inline spinners/PowerBait/worm rigs for trout.

## Lake Cle Elum

Context:

- Prior discussion treated it as cold-water trout/salmonid reservoir, not a bass target.

Prior guidance:

- Fish trout/lake trout/kokanee-type patterns, not bass.
- Shore tactics: spoons, inline spinners, bait/worm rigs early/late near inflows/points/steep shore.
