## Practical fishing implications

Because the kayak is currently paddle-only and lacks anchoring/drift-control accessories, fishing plans should emphasize:

- moving routes rather than stationary spots;
- slow trolling passes;
- drifting along weed edges, bench edges, and contour lines;
- casting ahead of the kayak before drifting over fish;
- using Navionics to plan routes and the Helix to confirm depth, weeds, bait, and bottom changes.

Avoid plans that depend on hovering precisely over a waypoint for long periods unless conditions are calm.
