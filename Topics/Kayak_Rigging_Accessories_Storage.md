## Rod-holder issue

Historical chats include a photo/discussion of installed rear flush-mounted rod holders. The issue: the tubes angle such that rods lean forward and get in the way when paddling.

Discussed possibilities included:

- removing and rotating rod holders 180 degrees;
- concern that 180-degree rotation could make rods lean too far rearward due to deck panel angle;
- heat-bending the tubes with a heat gun and broom handle;
- considering other ways to change rod angle.

**Status:** unresolved. Do not treat heat-bending as approved/completed.
