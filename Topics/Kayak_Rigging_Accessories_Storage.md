## Rod-holder issue

Historical chats include a photo/discussion of the installed [Pelican flush-mounted rod holders](https://www.confluenceoutdoor.com/en-ca/products/bright-orange-flush-mount-rod-holder/PS1611). The issue: the tubes angle such that rods lean forward and get in the way when paddling.

Discussed possibilities included:

- removing and rotating rod holders 180 degrees;
- concern that 180-degree rotation could make rods lean too far rearward due to deck panel angle;
- heat-bending the tubes with a heat gun and broom handle;
- considering other ways to change rod angle.

**Status:** unresolved. Do not treat heat-bending as approved/completed.

## Loading checklist — canonical procedure seed

1. Move truck to middle of driveway, open bed cover and tailgate, place ground pad behind bed, move garbage bins.
2. Open orange bin and remove cart and cam straps; put bin, cam straps, and tackle bags in truck bed.
3. Put in back seat: backpack with polarized glasses, extra clothes, first-aid kit, sunscreen, Pelican case; Yeti bag with food, water, and ice; water shoes.
4. Assemble cart with bunk bases spanning from 100 to 200 on axle tube.
5. Unlock kayak, remove cover and put in garage.
6. Remove seat, paddle, and dry box and put in truck.
7. Remove cam straps from bag, close tailgate, and put straps on back of truck.
8. Remove scupper plugs (8) and orange ball and put in garage.
9. Make sure rudder is up and slide kayak onto cart, with cart to front of kayak.
10. Position kayak with stern on ground pad, bow 45° toward driver's side.
11. Lift bow and place on bunk ends.
12. Lift stern and slide kayak forward until rear scupper hole is above bend in bunks.
13. Attach rear cam strap, then side straps, then front strap.
14. Put ground pad, cart, and rods in truck.
15. Verify that orange bin, tackle bags, kayak seat, paddle, foam pad, ground pad, and cart are in truck bed; rods are in the tube; backpack, Yeti bag, dry box, and water shoes are in back seat.
16. Close tailgate and bed cover, move garbage bins back into place.
