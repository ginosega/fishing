### General spring positioning

Lake Sammamish is smaller and more manageable than Lake Washington, warms faster, has more vegetation, and largemouth are generally easier to find in spring. Prespawn fish transition between winter staging areas and emerging weed edges, shallow warming flats, and docks adjacent to deeper water. North-end shallows often warm before the main basin.

### North end

- Best spring area due to shallow warming water, Sammamish River influence, vegetation, docks, protected water, largemouth concentration.
- Launch: Idylwood Park.
- Fish afternoon on warm sunny days, starting on outside weed edge or first break, then moving shallower as afternoon warms.
- Targets:
  - isolated docks adjacent to weed edges and 6-12 ft darker-bottom areas;
  - emerging weed edges/new growth/submerged grass in 4-10 ft;
  - river-mouth transition where current meets lake and shallow meets basin.
- Use jerkbaits, swimbaits, finesse rigs.

### South end

- Launch: Lake Sammamish State Park.
- Can be excellent later in spring; early prespawn is timing-dependent.
- Fish edges of weed flats near deeper water and docks near Issaquah Creek.

### East shoreline

- Steeper banks, docks, transition zones, scattered rock.
- More smallmouth-oriented, similar to Lake Washington.
- Use jerkbait around rocky transitions, points, and dock edges; Ned rig around rock-sand transitions; small 3-4" swimbaits.

### User observation / catch

- 2026-08-04: 10" perch and 12" largemouth at Lake Sammamish; kayak fishing over a bench west of Tibbetts Beach on south end; caught in <10 ft along edge of cover on Strike King KVD Square Bill 1.0 crankbait.
- Historical observation: possible smallmouth spawning/juvenile area about half mile north of State Park launch on east side, around 12 ft flat dropping on three sides; larger fish may stage on adjacent deeper edges.

## Before fishing

For every trip, recheck:

- Fish Washington regulations.
- Emergency rules.
- Park/dock signage.
- Parking/launch restrictions.
- Species-specific retention rules.
