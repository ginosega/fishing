Pass the tag end of the line through the eye twice. Wrap it around the standing end five or six times. Thread the end through the original loop beside the eye. Lubricate and pull the knot tight. Trim the end, but leave about an eighth of an inch for security.

**Uses:** The double wrap of line through the eye makes the Trilene knot a strong and reliable knot to join monofilament line to hooks, swivels and lures. It resists slippage and failures and is an alternative to the improved clinch knot.
