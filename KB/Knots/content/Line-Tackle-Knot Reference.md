**Braided line**
- To leader: FG knot, or Albright when on the water
- To swivel: N/A (the swivel is in the leader)
- To snap: Palomar
- To hook: N/A (use leader)
- To lure: Palomar or Modified Uni (topwater fishing only, otherwise use leader)

**Fluorocarbon**
- To swivel: Trilene
- To snap: Trilene
- To hook: Improved Clinch, or Palomar for heavier tackle
- To lure: Use snap for more action, otherwise Palomar

**Monofilament**
- To swivel: Improved Clinch
- To snap: Improved Clinch
- To hook: Improved Clinch, or Palomar for heavier tackle
- To lure: Use snap for more action, otherwise Palomar
