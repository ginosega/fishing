The non-slip loop knot is a good knot for crankbaits that do not have a split ring attached to their eye because it allows for more side-to-side action of the bait than a palomar or modified uni knot that cinches directly to the eye.

When using any loop knot with a lure, the knot must be inspected regularly for wear or fraying of the loop.
