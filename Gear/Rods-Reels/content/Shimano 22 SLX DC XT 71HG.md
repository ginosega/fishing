Primary general-purpose bass setup for heavier lures, stronger hooksets, and more accurate target casting.

Current line: [PowerPro Super8 Slick V2 30 lb Moss Green](gear://powerpro-super8-slick-v2-30) braid with [Seaguar InvizX 12 lb](gear://seaguar-invizx-12) fluorocarbon leader.

Best suited to spinnerbaits, chatterbaits, crankbaits, jigs, swimbaits, topwater, and other presentations where the spinning setup is lighter than ideal.

For the SLX DC XT, set the internal line-type dial for the line being used and adjust braking for lure weight, wind, and casting conditions. Thumb the spool at splashdown to help prevent backlash.
